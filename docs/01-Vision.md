# 01 — Vision

## Company Vision
Yample Labs builds AuditAI: an AI-driven website auditing platform for agencies and enterprises that combines performance, SEO, accessibility, security, and business insights.

## Product Vision
Provide a single-pane audit experience that delivers technical diagnostics plus AI-generated business recommendations and proposal assets suitable for agency workflows.

## Mission
Help agencies and product teams quickly understand website health and unlock revenue opportunities through automated audits and AI-assisted proposals.

## Target Users & Personas
- Agency Owner: needs proposals and revenue estimates.
- Technical Lead: needs actionable, prioritized fixes.
- Product Manager: needs conversion & UX insights.
- Marketing Manager: needs SEO and content recommendations.

## Business Model & Pricing
- SaaS subscription tiers: Starter, Pro, Agency, Enterprise.
- Usage-based billing for audits (credits), plus seat-based pricing.
